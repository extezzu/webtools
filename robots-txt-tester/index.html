robots.txt tester

Paste your robots.txt and the URLs or paths you want to test. Matching follows Google's rules: the most specific (longest) rule wins, Allow wins ties, and * and $ wildcards are supported.

Testing a whole site? Our Sitemap URL Validator checks every URL in your sitemap and flags pages that are blocked, noindexed, redirected or broken.

Common robots.txt mistakes

  • Disallow: / left over from a staging site blocks everything.
  • Blocking CSS/JS files stops Google from rendering pages properly.
  • robots.txt does not remove pages from Google: use noindex for that, and don't block the page or Google can't see the noindex.
  • Rules are case-sensitive for paths.
