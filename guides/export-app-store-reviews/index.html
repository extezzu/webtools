How to export App Store reviews from all countries

App Store Connect shows reviews one country at a time and has no "export everything" button. If you want all reviews of your app (or a competitor's) in one spreadsheet, here are the options.

Why one country is not enough

Apple keeps reviews per storefront. A US-only export of a global app can miss most of its feedback: German users complain about different things than Brazilian users. Apple's public review feed also returns only the latest ~500 reviews per country, so walking every storefront is the only way to get a big sample.

Option 1: App Store Connect (your own apps only)

  1. Open App Store Connect → your app → Ratings and Reviews.
  2. Pick a country from the dropdown and scroll.
  3. Copy reviews by hand, or use the App Store Connect API (customerReviews endpoint) with an API key.

Fine for one app and a few countries; slow for anything else, and it doesn't work for competitors.

Option 2: Apple's public RSS feed (any app, one country)

Apple publishes recent reviews as JSON at https://itunes.apple.com/{country}/rss/customerreviews/page={1-10}/id={appId}/sortby=mostrecent/json. The app id is the number after id in the App Store URL. You get 50 reviews per page and up to 10 pages per country. You'd need a script to loop over pages and countries and convert the JSON to CSV.

Option 3: one-click export for all countries (no code)

Our App Store Reviews Scraper (multi-country) does the looping for you:

  1. Paste App Store URLs, app ids or just app names.
  2. Set countries to top (18 big markets) or all (~100 storefronts).
  3. Optional: max stars = 2 to collect only complaints, keywords like crash, refund, login.
  4. Click Start, then download CSV, Excel or JSON.

It costs about $0.40 per 1,000 reviews, and reviews you filter out are free. Apify's free plan includes monthly credit that covers a few thousand reviews.

Monitor new reviews every day

Set sinceDate and create a daily schedule. Each run returns only reviews newer than the date, so you can send new 1-star reviews to Slack, Google Sheets or email through Apify integrations.

What to do with the export

  • Sort by app version to see if a release made ratings drop.
  • Group complaints by keyword (price, ads, crash, login) to rank fixes.
  • Paste the CSV into an LLM and ask for the top 10 problems per country.
