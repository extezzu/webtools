Open Graph & meta tag generator

Fill in the fields to get ready-to-paste <head> tags for search results, Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack and X (Twitter). Length checks follow common display limits: about 60 characters for titles and 160 for descriptions.

Auditing many pages? Our Sitemap URL Validator checks every URL in your sitemap for status codes, redirects, noindex and canonical problems.

Tips

  • Use absolute URLs (with https://) for og:image and og:url; relative paths are ignored by most crawlers.
  • Images of 1200×630 px work on Facebook, LinkedIn and X's large card; keep them under 5 MB.
  • After changing tags, platforms cache old previews: re-scrape with their debug tools.
  • Keep the title tag and og:title similar so search and social match.
