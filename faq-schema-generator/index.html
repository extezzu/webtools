FAQ schema generator

Write one question per block: the question on the first line, the answer on the following lines, and a blank line between items. You get valid FAQPage JSON-LD to paste in your page's <head> or body, and optional matching HTML.

Check the whole site: run our Sitemap URL Validator to find broken or noindexed pages before adding more markup.

Things to know

  • The questions and answers in the markup must also be visible on the page, or the markup breaks Google's guidelines.
  • Since 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for well-known government and health sites, so for most sites the markup no longer gives expandable results in Google Search. It is still valid schema.org, and other engines and AI assistants can read it.
  • Do not use FAQPage for forums or user-submitted answers (use QAPage), or for advertising.
  • Test the result with Google's Rich Results Test or the Schema Markup Validator.
