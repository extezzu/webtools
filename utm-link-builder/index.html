Naming rules that keep reports clean

  • UTM values are case-sensitive: Email and email show up as two rows. Pick lowercase and stick to it.
  • GA4 groups traffic into default channels using utm_medium (and source). Use standard mediums like email, cpc, social, referral, affiliate, or traffic lands in "Unassigned".
  • Never use UTMs on internal links between your own pages: they start a new session and overwrite the real source.
  • Keep a shared sheet of campaign names so the team reuses the same spelling.
